- `robinhood.jpg`: unmodified Robinhood_Avatar.jpg from the [Robinhood Chain brand asset archive](https://cdn.robinhood.com/robinhood_chain/brand_assets/robinhood-chain-brand-assets-v1.zip), linked by [Robinhood Chain brand guidelines](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/brand-guidelines/).

Other tokens use neutral monogram badges; other networks use a generic network glyph. The account endpoint supplies no verified logo URLs. Token badges are not issuer or endorsement claims. Assets are bundled, with no runtime image requests.
